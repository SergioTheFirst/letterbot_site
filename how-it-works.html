How LetterBot works

Last updated: March 2026

Direct answer: LetterBot adds a local decision layer over email: fetch from IMAP, extract useful facts, assign priority, suggest action, deliver to Telegram, and preserve enough evidence to explain what happened.

1. Fetch

The worker polls configured IMAP mailboxes and detects new messages.

2. Extract

Sender, subject, body, and useful attachment content are normalized into a structured interpretation.

3. Classify

Mail type, urgency cues, business signals, and action hints are evaluated.

4. Prioritize

The system chooses a final priority and can compare heuristic, AI, and shadow signals.

5. Deliver

Telegram receives a compact card with controls for manual correction and snooze.

6. Observe

The local dashboard and traces expose health, decision evidence, and runtime behavior.

This matters because a strong inbox assistant is not just a summarizer. It is a workflow with visible states, graceful degradation, and auditable decisions.
