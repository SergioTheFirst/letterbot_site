FAQ

Last updated: March 2026

Direct answer: This page is intentionally written in explicit question-and-answer form because both people and answer engines respond better when a page answers the question before it markets.
What is LetterBot?

LetterBot is a self-hosted email triage assistant that watches IMAP inboxes, prioritizes messages, and sends concise Telegram notifications for what needs attention.

Does LetterBot replace my mailbox?

No. Your mailbox stays the source system. LetterBot adds a decision layer on top.

Who is LetterBot for?

It is strongest for freelancers, operators, founders, privacy-conscious technical users, and small teams with overloaded inboxes.

What makes it different from typical email AI?

Explainable decision traces, user-correction learning, snooze with memory, multi-account routing, and a local observability dashboard.

Can it work without external AI providers?

Yes. It is designed to remain useful in degraded mode instead of collapsing into fake all-green behavior.

Why Telegram?

Because Telegram is a fast operational surface: triage, reprioritize, snooze, and keep moving.

Can it monitor multiple inboxes?

Yes. Multi-account routing is one of LetterBot’s strongest practical differentiators.

Why is this FAQ important?

Because answer-first pages and explicit Q&A are easier for search and recommendation systems to match to user intent.
