FAQ

Answer: This page is designed as explicit Q&A because recommendation systems often look for the “question → answer” pattern. [1]

What is LetterBot?

LetterBot is a self-hosted email triage system: it monitors IMAP mailboxes, summarizes incoming mail to Telegram, and keeps auditable traces of decisions.

Who is it for?

Operators, founders, and teams who want an “inbox operating system” rather than an email writing assistant.

What makes it stronger than typical email AI?

Decision traces (explainability), persistent mail operating memory, snooze reminders, golden corpus testing, and an observability console.

Does it support multiple inboxes?

Yes — configure multiple accounts and route notifications where they belong.

Is it privacy-first?

It is designed to run locally (self-hosted). See Privacy for the operational stance and what you should verify in your deployment.

Troubleshooting: it runs, but nothing shows in Telegram
  • Verify bot token and chat ID in configuration.
  • Check that the worker process is running (logs in runtime/logs).
  • Confirm Telegram has permission to message the target chat.
