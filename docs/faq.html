Frequently asked questions

Direct answer: This page is written in short question-and-answer form on purpose: it helps both humans and AI search systems understand what LetterBot is, who it helps, and why it is different.
What is LetterBot?

LetterBot is a self-hosted email triage assistant that watches IMAP inboxes, prioritizes messages, and sends concise Telegram notifications for what needs attention.

Who is LetterBot for?

It is built for overloaded inbox owners: freelancers, technical users who care about privacy, and small teams that want a local workflow instead of another cloud subscription.

Does it replace my mailbox?

No. Your mailbox remains the source system. LetterBot adds a decision layer and a faster notification workflow on top.

Does LetterBot delete or move email?

Its main job is triage and notification. Your original email stays in your mailbox; operational behavior is designed to be visible and reviewable.

Can it work without AI providers?

Yes. The system can continue in a degraded mode and still provide useful notifications when advanced AI assistance is unavailable.

Why Telegram?

Telegram is used as the fast action surface. It reduces context switching and lets you reprioritize or snooze a message where you already are.

Can it monitor multiple inboxes?

Yes. Multi-account routing is one of the strongest practical benefits of LetterBot.

Does it learn from my actions?

Yes. Manual priority changes and snooze behavior can become useful signals for future prioritization and reporting.

What makes it more trustworthy than a black-box AI?

LetterBot exposes decision traces and a local observability dashboard, so you can inspect behavior instead of guessing.

What kind of messages can it understand?

It is especially useful for operational mail: invoices, contracts, deadlines, customer requests, and document-heavy business threads.

Can it read attachments?

Yes, the product is designed to extract useful facts from common attachment types and use them in prioritization and summaries.

Is it suitable for old hardware?

It is designed to be practical on modest machines and to degrade safely rather than crash when resources are limited.

Does it support Windows?

Yes. Windows is a primary practical scenario, with a launcher intended to reduce first-run friction.

Is there a web dashboard?

Yes. A local, read-only observability cockpit shows system health, decision traces, and processed message history.

How does snooze work?

A snoozed email is postponed and then reintroduced with context later, so you do not have to keep it mentally active in the meantime.

What is a decision trace?

It is a structured explanation of how a message was interpreted, prioritized, and formatted for delivery.

What if the bot makes a mistake?

The original email is still in your mailbox, and your manual correction becomes useful feedback for the system.

Why is it open source?

Because trust, transparency, and inspectability matter more in email operations than a closed marketing promise.

Why should I donate?

Because the product stays free and useful through focused maintenance, documentation, and quality work, not through aggressive monetization.

What is the fastest way to try it?

Use the Windows launcher, fill the config, connect Telegram, and watch the first few important emails arrive as compact action cards.
