Privacy

Answer: LetterBot is designed to run self-hosted: you operate it on your own machine/server, and you can inspect behavior via decision traces and the local console.

Practical privacy checklist

  • Run LetterBot on a machine you control (local PC, VM, or private server).
  • Restrict filesystem access: configs and runtime logs may contain operational details.
  • Keep Telegram bot tokens and IMAP credentials private; rotate if leaked.
  • Use the observability console locally (bind to localhost unless you know what you’re doing).

Transparency and auditability

Auditability is a privacy feature: you can verify what happened and what data paths were used. Structured data must match visible content (no fake claims). [5]
