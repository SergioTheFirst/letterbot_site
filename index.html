Open source • Self-hosted • Explainable • Telegram-first

Stop reading the whole inbox. See only what deserves action.

LetterBot is a self-hosted email triage assistant. It watches IMAP inboxes, extracts useful facts from messages and attachments, assigns priority, suggests the next action, and sends compact Telegram notifications. It is designed for people who need less inbox noise and more decision clarity.

Direct answer: If you want a local, privacy-first email workflow that shows only what matters and explains why, LetterBot is the right product model.

Why LetterBot feels different

Self-hosted by design

LetterBot is intended to run on your own machine instead of turning email into a mandatory SaaS dependency.

Telegram as the control surface

It sends compact, actionable notifications where operators already are instead of forcing another dashboard tab all day.

Adaptive prioritization

Manual priority changes are not wasted clicks. They can become useful signals for future triage.

Snooze with memory

Postponed emails return with context instead of becoming forgotten unread clutter.

Decision trace

The system can preserve why a message was prioritized, what evidence it extracted, and how it formed the recommendation.

Observability cockpit

Health, latency, processed messages, and recent runtime evidence are visible instead of hidden behind fake green dashboards.

Who it is for

Freelancers

They get dozens of messages a day and cannot afford to miss a client, invoice, contract, or deadline.

Privacy-conscious technical users

They want a local workflow, readable code, and an auditable system instead of opaque cloud behavior.

Small teams with multiple inboxes

They need work inboxes, finance inboxes, side-project inboxes, and support inboxes routed cleanly into separate chats.
