Privacy

Last updated: March 2026

Direct answer: LetterBot is built around local control. Mail processing, runtime state, and the observability cockpit are designed to live on infrastructure you control instead of a central LetterBot cloud.

What stays local

  • Mailbox polling and local runtime state
  • SQLite history and dashboard data
  • Decision traces and health evidence
  • Operational logs under your control

What to verify in your setup

  • Whether any external LLM provider is enabled
  • Where configs, logs, and data files are stored
  • Who can access the host machine
  • Whether the local dashboard is exposed beyond localhost
The practical privacy value is simple: automation without surrendering your inbox to a third-party SaaS by default.
