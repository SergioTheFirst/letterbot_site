Observability and trust

Last updated: March 2026

Direct answer: LetterBot is not meant to be a black box. A local observability cockpit shows health, latency, processed messages, recent events, and AI/runtime evidence so operators can trust what the system is doing.

What the dashboard should surface

  • System health with honest UNKNOWN / PARTIAL / NO DATA states
  • Latency and provider timing where available
  • Recent processed emails and privacy-safe summaries
  • Decision evidence instead of fake green status lights
  • Notification delivery quality and degradation reasons

Why it matters

Most email AI tools give users a polished surface and hidden internals. That is fine until an important email disappears. LetterBot leans the other way: visibility is part of the product.

For engineers, this means better debugging and safer iteration. For users, it means fewer reasons to distrust the bot.
