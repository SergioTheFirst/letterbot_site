How to install

1

Download & unzip

Download the ZIP above. Unzip to any folder on your PC — for example C:\letterbot. No installer required.

2

Configure

Open accounts.yaml and fill in your IMAP credentials and Telegram bot token. See the README inside the archive for provider-specific setup (Gmail, Yandex, Mail.ru, Exchange).

3

Run

Double-click letterbot.bat. Letterbot starts polling your inbox. Telegram notifications arrive on the next check cycle.

💻

System requirements

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
  • IMAP access to your mailbox
  • Telegram bot token (from @BotFather, free)
  • ~200 MB free disk space
  • ~1 GB free RAM (tested on Celeron, 3 GB total)
  • Internet access for IMAP and Telegram
