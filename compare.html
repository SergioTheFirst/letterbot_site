When Letterbot is the right choice

  • You want Telegram notifications — not another app to open
  • Privacy matters: you don't want email processed in someone else's cloud
  • You have a spare PC or old laptop to repurpose
  • Multiple inboxes → multiple Telegram chats
  • Open source is important — you want to read the code
  • No budget for subscriptions, but willing to invest setup time
  • Russian LLM (GigaChat) is preferred for compliance reasons

When to look at alternatives

  • You need enterprise SLA, compliance certs, or corporate support contracts
  • You want a full email client, not just a Telegram notifier — try Superhuman
  • You need real-time team collaboration on email — try Missive
  • Technical setup is a barrier and you need a zero-config tool
  • You need native iOS/Android app (Letterbot works via Telegram)
  • You're already deep in Gmail ecosystem and Google privacy is acceptable
