Open source • Self-hosted • Privacy-first • Explainable

See only the email that matters — with Telegram summaries, explainable decisions, and local control.

LetterBot is a self-hosted email triage assistant for overloaded inboxes. It reads incoming mail through IMAP, decides what matters, sends compact Telegram notifications, and keeps a local audit trail so you can see why a decision was made.

Direct answer: If you want an email assistant that works on your own machine, keeps control in your hands, and helps you react to important messages faster, LetterBot is built for that job.

Why LetterBot feels different

Most email AI products optimize for writing email. LetterBot optimizes for operating the inbox: triage, prioritization, reminders, accountability, and visibility into how decisions were made.

Explainable AI

Decision trace, not a black box

Inspect why a message was marked urgent, what evidence was extracted, and which path the system used.

Behavioral adaptation

It learns from your corrections

Manual priority changes and snooze history become useful signals instead of disappearing into the void.

Operational workflow

Telegram as an action console

Act on important email where you already are: review, reprioritize, snooze, and continue working.

Privacy

Your machine stays in control

The product is designed for local operation and can keep working in a degraded mode even when AI providers are unavailable.

Scale

Multiple inboxes, one workflow

Monitor many accounts and route notifications to the right Telegram chats without turning everything into one giant noisy feed.

Trust

Observability built in

A local dashboard shows health, latency, recent decisions, and processed email history instead of fake green status badges.

Who uses LetterBot

Freelancers and solo operators

They do not want to miss a paying client because a critical email drowned in newsletters and receipts.

Technical users with privacy requirements

They want a self-hosted workflow, GitHub-visible code, and predictable behavior they can inspect.

Small businesses with multiple inboxes

They need one lightweight assistant to route important mail across finance, support, founder, and personal accounts.

Three-step setup

1

Install locally

Use the Windows launcher or run with Python. The first start prepares the config files and points you to the right place.

2

Connect email and Telegram

Add IMAP credentials and your Telegram bot settings. Multi-account routing is supported.

3

Review only what matters

LetterBot parses, prioritizes, and sends concise Telegram summaries with optional snooze and manual correction.

What makes it unusually strong

Adaptive prioritization

Snooze intelligence
