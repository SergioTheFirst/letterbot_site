I stopped reading my email the old way

~8 min read · open source · Python · Telegram

Direct answer: LetterBot was born from a simple pain: too much time spent opening email just to discover what actually matters.

At some point the problem stopped being “email is annoying” and became “email is stealing focused work.” The first instinct was rules and filters. They helped a little, then collapsed under real-world context. The next instinct was to hand everything to a giant language model. That was also wrong.

The result was a different design: deterministic extraction where possible, AI assistance where useful, and Telegram as the operational interface instead of another inbox tab. The interesting part is not just that LetterBot summarizes email. The interesting part is that it explains why something was important, lets the user correct it, and gets better signals from those corrections.

That is the real product idea: not a mailbox clone, not a writing assistant, but a personal decision layer over email.

When the workflow works, you stop “processing email” and start reacting to what deserves your attention.
