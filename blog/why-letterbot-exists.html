Why LetterBot exists

Updated: March 2026 • Product essay

Direct answer: LetterBot exists because inbox overload is not a reading problem. It is a decision problem.

Rules helped a little. Generic AI summaries helped a little. Neither solved the actual question: what deserves attention now, and why?

LetterBot takes a different route. It treats the inbox as an operational system. It watches mailboxes, extracts useful facts, assigns priority, suggests the next action, and exposes enough evidence for a human to trust or correct the outcome.

That combination matters because a useful inbox assistant should not just summarize. It should help people decide.
