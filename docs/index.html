Why LetterBot is different

Most email “AI assistants” optimize for writing emails. LetterBot optimizes for operating the inbox: triage, routing, reminders, and inspection of *why* something happened.

Explainable AI, not magic

Every decision is inspectable: signals, score, logic path, and what changed in storage.

Memory that matters

A persistent “mail operating memory” enables stable behavior across repeated patterns and threads.

Snooze is a first-class behavior

Postpone with intent: keep it out of the inbox, but never lose it.

Built for privacy-first setups

Self-hosted by default. Your mailbox traffic stays under your control.

Golden corpus testing

Evaluate changes against a fixed set of examples so improvements are measurable.

Observability console

See what the system did, when, and why — without digging through random logs.

How it works

  1. Connect inboxes: configure IMAP + Telegram settings once.
  2. LetterBot listens: new mail triggers parsing + triage.
  3. Telegram output: minimal actions, priorities, and next steps.
  4. Audit anytime: open the local console and inspect decision traces.
