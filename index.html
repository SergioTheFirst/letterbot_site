What is Letterbot?
Letterbot is a free, self-hosted AI email filter for Windows that connects to your IMAP mailbox, analyzes incoming emails, determines priority and suggested action, and sends concise Telegram notifications — only for messages that need your attention.
Who it's for: Freelancers and sole traders who manage 20+ clients by email. Developers who want self-hosted, auditable tools. Small businesses who want email intelligence without sending data to foreign clouds.
🔒

Your data stays on your machine

Letterbot has no cloud servers. Email metadata is stored in a local SQLite database. No telemetry, no analytics, nothing sent to Letterbot. You can verify this in the open-source code.

📲

Telegram is your inbox dashboard

One email → one Telegram message with priority emoji, summary, suggested action, and response buttons. No extra app to open. Works on every device with Telegram installed.

$0

Free forever — seriously

AGPL-3.0 open source. No freemium, no feature paywall, no subscription tier. The project is sustained by voluntary donations from users who find it useful. That's the whole model.

How does Letterbot work?

1

Reads your inbox via IMAP

Connects to your mail server in read-only mode. Never modifies, deletes, or moves any email. Supports Yandex, Mail.ru, Gmail, Exchange, and any IMAP provider.

2

Analyzes and prioritizes

Extracts sender, subject, body, attachments. Determines priority (🔴 Urgent / 🟡 Important / 🔵 Low) and suggested action. Works fully offline without external AI.

3

Sends to Telegram

One message per email with priority, summary, action suggestion, and buttons. You can change priority or snooze directly in Telegram. System learns from your corrections.

Does Letterbot require ChatGPT or any paid AI? −
No. Letterbot runs in deterministic mode without any external AI by default. GigaChat, OpenAI, and Cloudflare Workers AI are all optional — you activate one explicitly in the config file if you want higher accuracy on unusual texts.
Where do my emails go? +
Nowhere external. Letterbot reads email via IMAP directly from your mail server. Processing happens locally. The SQLite database is on your machine. There are no Letterbot servers.
Is it really free forever? +
Yes. Letterbot is free and open source under AGPL-3.0. There is no freemium model, no "Pro" tier, no subscription. The project runs on voluntary donations via Boosty from users who find it useful.
Does it work on an old or slow computer? +
Yes — tested on an Intel Celeron N4020 (2 cores, 1.1 GHz) with 3 GB RAM. Giving an old laptop a second life as a local email assistant is one of the best Letterbot use cases.
