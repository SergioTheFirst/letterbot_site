Privacy

Direct answer: LetterBot is built around local control. The practical privacy promise is simple: keep mailbox processing on infrastructure you control, expose what the system is doing, and avoid silent cloud dependence.

What stays local

  • Mailbox polling and local runtime state
  • SQLite history and local web cockpit data
  • Decision traces and internal health evidence

What to verify in your own setup

  • Where your configuration files and logs are stored
  • Whether an external LLM provider is enabled
  • What minimal context is sent when a provider is used
  • Who can access the local machine and dashboard
