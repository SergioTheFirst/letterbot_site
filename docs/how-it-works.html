How LetterBot works

Direct answer: LetterBot runs a local pipeline: fetch email via IMAP, extract the useful facts, decide priority and next action, deliver a compact Telegram card, and store enough evidence to explain the decision later.
1

Fetch

The worker polls configured IMAP inboxes and detects new messages.

2

Extract

The system reads sender, subject, body, and useful attachment content.

3

Interpret

Facts such as amounts, dates, document type, and action cues are assembled.

4

Prioritize

LetterBot decides whether the message is low, medium, or high priority.

5

Deliver

A short Telegram card shows the important part, not the entire inbox noise.

6

Observe

The local dashboard and decision trace expose what happened and why.

Why the pipeline matters

A premium inbox assistant is not just a parser. It is a sequence of stable steps with explicit degradation paths, visible health, and auditable output.

  • Provider unavailable? The system degrades honestly instead of pretending everything is green.
  • Attachment too noisy? The pipeline still delivers a useful summary and keeps the original email intact.
  • User disagrees? A correction becomes a signal, not a dead-end click.

What users experience

The user does not need to understand the entire pipeline. They need a reliable result: an actionable Telegram card and enough trust to rely on it in daily work.

LetterBot is best described as a decision layer over email, not as a generic chatbot for the inbox.
