Why Letterbot exists

The case for local, free, privacy-first email AI — written by the developer. This is an argument, not a product page.

Why build another email tool?
Because every existing option that does AI email analysis sends your correspondence to a cloud server in the United States. For personal email about weekends and restaurants, that is acceptable. For business email containing client names, contract terms, invoice amounts, and negotiation positions — that is a meaningful risk that most users accept because they have no alternative. Letterbot exists to be that alternative.
What is the actual privacy risk with cloud email AI?
The risk is not that Gmail or Superhuman will actively misuse your data. The risk is structural: any system where your business correspondence is stored on foreign servers creates a legal, operational, and competitive exposure. That exposure may never materialize. But it is real, and it grows as the volume of sensitive correspondence increases.
A sole trader's client list, pricing conversations, and contract negotiation history in a foreign cloud database is a different threat model than that same data on a machine under their desk. Letterbot is designed for people who have decided that difference matters.
Why free? What is the catch?
There is no catch. The decision to make Letterbot free is a deliberate choice rooted in the positioning: a privacy-first tool monetized by ads or data collection is self-contradictory. A freemium model with locked features punishes exactly the users who are most price-sensitive — the freelancers and small businesses who need the tool most.
The honest model: build something genuinely useful, release it free and open-source, sustain it through voluntary donations from users who find it valuable. This creates a smaller but more committed user base than a freemium funnel, and it preserves the project's integrity. Development is funded by donations via Boosty. That is the whole model.
Is a local tool always worse than a cloud tool?
For features that require continuous learning across millions of users — spam detection, suggested replies, smart search — yes, cloud wins. For features that require only local knowledge — who matters to you, what patterns in your correspondence are unusual, what your weekly commitment obligations look like — local is not just acceptable, it is better. Letterbot is built around the latter category.
Additionally, the local model creates a different relationship between user and tool: the user owns their data, controls the software version, and can inspect every decision the system makes. For technical users, this is not a compromise — it is a feature.

The one-line version

Your business correspondence deserves to stay in your building. Letterbot is the tool that makes that possible without sacrificing email intelligence.
