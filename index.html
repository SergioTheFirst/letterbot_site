What is Letterbot?

Letterbot is a free, self-hosted email filter for Windows that connects to your IMAP mailbox, analyzes incoming emails, determines priority and suggested action, and sends concise Telegram notifications — only for messages that need your attention.

Who it's for: Freelancers and sole traders who manage 20+ clients by email. Developers who want self-hosted, auditable tools. Small businesses who want email intelligence without sending data to foreign clouds.

🔒

Your data stays on your machine

Letterbot has no cloud servers. Email metadata is stored in a local SQLite database. No telemetry, no analytics, nothing sent to Letterbot. You can verify this in the open-source code.

📲

Telegram is your inbox dashboard

One email → one Telegram message with priority emoji, summary, suggested action, and response buttons. No extra app to open. Works on every device with Telegram installed.

$0

Free forever — seriously

AGPL-3.0 open source. No freemium, no feature paywall, no subscription tier. The project is sustained by voluntary donations from users who find it useful.

How does Letterbot work?

1

Reads your inbox via IMAP

Connects to your mail server in read-only mode. Never modifies, deletes, or moves any email. Supports Gmail, Yandex, Mail.ru, Outlook, and any IMAP provider. Unlimited accounts.

2

Analyzes and prioritizes

Extracts sender, subject, body, and attachments (PDF, Excel, Word). Determines priority (🔴 Urgent / 🟡 Important / 🔵 Low) and suggested action using a deterministic rules engine. Works fully offline without any external AI.

3

Sends to Telegram

One message per email with priority, summary, action suggestion, and inline buttons. You can change priority or snooze directly in Telegram. The system learns from your corrections over time.

Does Letterbot require ChatGPT or any paid AI?

No. Letterbot runs in deterministic mode without any external by default. GigaChat (free, by Sber) and Cloudflare Workers (free tier) are optional — you activate one explicitly in the config file if you want higher accuracy on complex texts. OpenAI is not required.

Where do my emails go?

Nowhere external. Letterbot reads email via IMAP directly from your mail server. Processing happens locally on your machine. The SQLite database is stored next to the executable. There are no Letterbot servers — they don't exist.

Is it really free forever?

Yes. Letterbot is free and open source under AGPL-3.0. There is no freemium model, no "Pro" tier, no subscription. The project runs on voluntary donations via Boosty from users who find it useful. All features are available to everyone.

Does it work on an old or slow computer?

Yes — tested and verified on an Intel Celeron N4020 (2 cores, 1.1 GHz) with 3 GB RAM running Windows 10. Uses 40–120 MB RAM in operation. Giving an old laptop a second life as a local email assistant is one of the best Letterbot use cases.

How many email accounts can I connect?

Unlimited. Add as many IMAP accounts as you need — each can be routed to a different Telegram chat. There is no account limit in Letterbot. Work, personal, client accounts — all in one bot, each going to the right chat.
