Letterbot is the right choice when…

  • You want Telegram notifications — not another app to check
  • Privacy matters: you don't want email processed in someone else's cloud
  • You have a spare PC or old laptop to repurpose
  • Multiple inboxes → multiple Telegram chats
  • Open source and code auditability are important
  • No budget for subscriptions but willing to invest 10 minutes of setup
  • Russian LLM (GigaChat) is preferred for compliance
→

Consider alternatives when…

  • You need enterprise SLA, compliance certification, centralized IT management
  • You want a full email client, not just a Telegram notifier → Superhuman
  • Team collaboration on email is the primary need → Missive
  • Technical setup is a hard barrier and you need zero-config
  • You need native iOS/Android app (Letterbot works through Telegram)
  • You are fully committed to Gmail and Google's privacy model is acceptable
