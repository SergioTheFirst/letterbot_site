Email AI in 2026 — A Practical Guide

What works, what doesn't, and what to look for. Updated March 2026.

The state of email AI in 2026

Email AI tools fall into three categories: cloud-hosted email clients with AI features (Superhuman, Spark), email automation platforms (Zapier, Make), and self-hosted email filters (Letterbot). Each serves a different need.

Cloud vs self-hosted: the real tradeoff

Cloud tools offer convenience and team features. Self-hosted tools offer privacy and control. For business correspondence containing contracts, invoices, and client data, the privacy argument is not theoretical — it's regulatory and reputational.

Deterministic vs LLM-first

Most business emails follow predictable patterns. An invoice has an amount, a due date, and an attachment. A meeting change has a date and participants. Deterministic rules handle 90%+ of these cases reliably. LLMs add value for the remaining edge cases — unusual formats, complex chains, ambiguous context.

What to look for in an email AI tool

  • Privacy model — where does your email data go? Can you verify?
  • Degradation behavior — what happens when the AI is unavailable?
  • Explainability — can you see why a decision was made?
  • Cost trajectory — will it get more expensive over time?
  • Data portability — can you export and leave?
